Image Style Guides  (1)
image-style-guide
Service Business Hero Images
service-business-hero
Subject: Clients in an "after" moment — speaking on stage, holding the finished product, in their transformed space.
Style: Confident, aspirational, warm. Lifestyle or environmental composition.
Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 3:1 for full-width; 4:3 for contained hero.
Overlay: Dark overlay at 40–60% opacity when text sits on image.
✓ Show the client outcome visually
✓ Good tonal range so overlays remain readable
✓ Subject off-center or looking inward to avoid text conflicts
✓ Oval/pill crops for portrait images in split layouts
✗ Never use the business owner or team
✗ No generic stock "business people" photos
✗ No closeup portrait when text overlays the image
✗ No busy backgrounds without dark overlay
✗ Don't use three images — third slot = stat badge
