## Visual Description
- Shape: full-width horizontal strip
- Size: ~40–48px tall
- Background: dark (near-black or very dark gray, # NEEDS CONFIRMATION)
- Border: none
- Border radius: 0 (flush to bottom edge of image container; may share image's bottom border radius)
- Shadow: none
- Typography: white text, ~13px, medium weight; checkmark icon (✓) before each item

## States
- Default: dark strip, white checkmark items
- Hover: n/a (static element)
- Active: n/a

## Usage Notes
- Anchored to the bottom edge of the hero image container — it is part of the image zone, not a separate section
- Always 3 items, evenly spaced (flex justify-around or space-between)
- Items are client OUTCOMES, not features, services, or process steps
- Examples: "✓ More Clients", "✓ Clear Authority", "✓ Bigger Opportunities"
- Wrong usage: "✓ 10 Years Experience", "✓ Free Consultation", "✓ Fast Turnaround"
