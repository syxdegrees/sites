## Visual Description
- Shape: rounded rectangle (less pill-like than the pure pill variants; border-radius ~8–12px)
- Size: medium — padding ~12px vertical, ~20px horizontal
- Background: dark (near-black or very dark navy)
- Border: none
- Border radius: ~8–12px
- Shadow: none observed
- Typography: white text, medium-bold weight, all-caps or title case; → arrow icon appended after label

## States
- Default: dark fill, white text + arrow
- Hover: unknown — likely slightly lighter
- Active: unknown

## Usage Notes
- Used on gradient/colored card backgrounds where a light button would disappear
- The → arrow signals a next step / forward momentum — suits action-oriented brands (fitness, coaching)
- Less playful than the pill shape — feels more direct and serious
- Used inside the gradient-card-left-panel as the hero CTA
- Also appears in the nav (as a more prominent CTA button variant with pill shape there)
