## Pattern
Results-focused headline with an accent highlight on the emotional payoff phrase.
Subheadline clarifies what you do + how it helps + removes objections, with selective bold on key phrases.

## Fields
| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| headline-line-1 | The setup — what the client gets (factual) | "Your Book. More Clients." |
| headline-line-2 | The payoff — the emotional outcome (highlighted) | "Real Authority." |
| subheadline | 2–3 sentences: what you do + how it helps + removes friction | "You've got the insights—let's turn them into a powerful book..." |
| subheadline-bold | Key phrases to bold within the subheadline | "powerful book", "your message, done right" |

## Rules
- Headline must be results-focused, not service-description ("Your Book. More Clients." not "Professional Ghostwriting Services")
- The accent highlight goes on the emotional payoff line only — never the whole headline
- Subheadline answers: what do you do, how does it help them, and why is it easy/safe to try
- Selective bold in subheadline draws the eye to the most important phrases — use 2–4 words max per bold instance
- Keep headline to 2 lines max; subheadline to 3 lines max at desktop widths

## Example
Headline line 1: Your Book. More Clients.
Headline line 2 (highlighted): Real Authority.
Subheadline: You've got the insights—**let's turn them into a powerful book** that brings in clients, builds your authority, and opens doors you didn't know existed. No writing stress, no second-guessing. Just **your message, done right.**
