How to read this
Left column = wireframe. Right column = numbered notes. Each grey block is a slot, not final content. Circled numbers on the wireframe map to the matching note. Notes describe structure only: width (full-bleed vs contained), approx height, column count and mobile collapse, sticky behavior, CTA hierarchy.

asset-list.md — the required-inputs checklist: fonts, colors, logos, images (with dimensions), icons, embeds, content data.
All three files share the section slugs: header · hero · promise · problem · cost-of-diy · guide · plan · transformation · proof · speed-to-results · offer · faq · features · final-cta · footer.

Notes — Hero

2

Hero — formatted as a landing page Full-bleed section, contained inner grid, ~70–85vh. Journey beat: Ordinary World + Call to Adventure.
Slots, in order: eyebrow — one line naming the ideal-client avatar (e.g. "For overworked teachers", "Houston homeowners") so the visitor knows the page is for them · headline (1–2 lines, promises the desired outcome / the problem solved — not a description of the service) · subhead (how you deliver on it; call out location if local) · two CTAs side by side — primary solid, secondary outline/ghost; primary is specific about what happens next · social-proof row directly beneath the CTAs: 5-star graphic + rating / "trusted by N" line, optional 3–5 small logo slots.
Visual area sits right of the copy on desktop (show the after-state / happy customer, never the work being done); stacks below copy on mobile with the primary CTA above the fold. This section must stand alone as a conversion unit even with nothing below it.

Notes — The Promise / Outcomes

3

The Promise / Outcomes Full-bleed section, short band. Journey beat: the vision of the transformed world — the promise stated up front, before the page dives into the problem.
Slots: single horizontal banner strip with exactly three outcomes evenly spaced across the full contained width, each = small icon + short outcome-label (one line, no supporting sentence). No dividers, no eyebrow, no border — flat "trusted by" treatment, not cards · directly below it, the client / partner logo wall (5 across desktop, wraps on mobile) — pairs the outcomes with who has actually achieved them.
Collapses to a vertical stack on mobile. No CTA. Distinct from Section 8 "The Transformation", which restates the same three outcomes later as full cards with supporting copy, after the proof.

Problem + Stakes Contained width. Journey beat: Refusal / the cost of staying put — names the pains, then what's at risk if nothing changes.

Notes — The Plan

7

The Plan Contained-wide. Journey beat: the map forward — makes the path feel simple and low-effort. People are terrified of complexity.
Slots: centered heading · exactly 3 steps — not 4, not 5 — each = step number + label ("Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3", never bare "1 / 2 / 3") + icon slot + step-title slot + one supporting line.
Step 1 = the client takes action — repeat the hero CTA verb. Step 2 = your whole process, simplified to its essence (you take the messy middle out of their hands). Step 3 = their happy outcome.
Row on desktop; on mobile switch to a vertical timeline (horizontal never works on a phone). Optional arrows / connecting timeline to make it feel like a journey, not a list. Optional low-commitment CTA centered beneath.

Notes — The Transformation

8

The Transformation Full-bleed section, contained-wide inner. Journey beat: crossing the threshold / futurecast — life after the problem is solved, framed as outcomes not features.
Treatment: alternating rows, not a card grid. Exactly three outcomes; each is a full-width row — visual block on one side, text on the other (title slot + 2–3 supporting lines), alternating left/right down the section. Reads substantial and editorial, distinct from the Plan grid above and the Proof wall below.
Below the rows: one "why us vs. the alternatives" line — how this stands out from competitors / existing offers.
On mobile: every row stacks visual-over-text. Substantiated restatement of Section 3 "The Promise" — same three outcomes, developed with copy and imagery.

Notes — Footer

15

Footer Full-bleed. Order: 4-col body → bottom bar.
Col 1: company logo (vertically aligned) · NAP citation block beneath it — business name, street address, city/state/zip, phone, business email — must match the Google Business Profile exactly (local-SEO citation consistency) · social media links beneath that (only platforms the business uses).
Col 2 — menu: About · Team · Contact · Careers.
Col 3 — menu: Services · Locations · Estimate · FAQs.
Col 4: embedded live Google Map · office hours beneath it · small "Visit Our Google Business Profile" button beneath that (exact button text).
Bottom bar (single row): © copyright line (left) · primary + secondary CTA, small, centered · legal menu (right) — Privacy · Terms · Disclaimers (+ similar items as needed).
4-col on desktop; stacks to 1-col on mobile in order: logo + citation + social → menu → menu → map + hours + GBP. Bottom-bar items stack on mobile.
