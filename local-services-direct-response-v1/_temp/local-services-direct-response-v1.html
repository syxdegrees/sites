HP7

The Plan Contained-wide. Journey beat: the map forward — makes the path feel simple and low-effort. People are terrified of complexity.
Slots: centered heading · exactly 3 steps — not 4, not 5 — each = step number + label ("Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3", never bare "1 / 2 / 3") + icon slot + step-title slot + one supporting line.

local-services-direct-response-v1  /  About

Page type: About  ·  Role: part of the Control template
Model: Modified sales page for the brand — a Hero's-Journey / StoryBrand arc told from the company's origin, every beat bent back to "…and that's why we're the right choice for you." Incorporates the Leader / Intro / Body credibility spine from skill-site-content-about.
Primary purpose: establish a credible backstory and show the visitor this is a real company with real people who care about them and their result — and that this business can help them specifically.
Sections: 12  ·  Body copy target: 800–1,200 words, H2/H3 structure.
Design direction (via frontend-design, then reconciled with the Home page): type carries most sections — a large headline stating the section's whole argument, with short grey supporting copy in a narrow column (< 66ch). Boxes are used sparingly, and where the About page needs a beat that also exists on the Home page (A7 values, A9 proof), it reuses the Home page's card treatment so the two pages flow together. Alignment: A2/A3/A5/A8/A10 left-aligned; A4/A6/A7/A9/A11 centered heading. The A4 milestone timeline is the one structural device, used only where the business has real dated milestones.
Notes on this page carry content intent as well as structure (it is copy-led). Colors, type, and final copy still come from the brand style guide + content brief.

About-page conventions
inline call = a click-to-call phone link woven into paragraph text (better than a button inside prose). Uses CallRail dynamic number insertion — see instructions.md "Phone numbers".
anchor text literal = the exact, keyword-optimized words a body link should use, matching its destination. Where the destination page doesn't exist yet it is written as plain text now and listed in the table below as a page to create.
Primary CTA (button) appears at the Leader, mid-page (Who We're For), and the closing band. No link stuffing — each internal link on this page is unique. Inline click-to-call links (CallRail DNI) appear in the prose at A3, A7, A10, A11 — but not stacked directly above a CTA button.

Notes — Where We Started

A4

Where We Started — ordinary world + call to adventure Full-bleed. Centered H2 origin heading, then the milestone timeline directly below it, then a left-aligned two-column block: a modest photo (fixed 260px, its own column with a real gutter) beside a narrow prose column (< 66ch) — not a 40/60 split panel, and the photo does not butt against the text. The photo is a founder shot, an early-days / on-the-job shot, or a team / premises shot, depending on mode.
Pick the mode that fits the business (the source skill assumes a founder; these fill the gap when there isn't one):
  1. Founder story — a real founder narrative: who they are, how they learned the trade, the moment they started. The skill-site-content-about "We've Grown Into a Trusted Name" pattern. Photo = the founder.
  2. Origin without a person — no single face: why this company exists — the market gap the owners saw, the standard they set, what they refuse to do. Photo = team or premises.
  3. New business as a strength — company is young: lean in. "We started [year] because…", a fresh approach, the experience the team brings, the owner's prior background. Honest about being new, framed as hungry and accountable.
Timeline sits directly below the H2: a horizontal hairline with dated nodes alternating above and below (collapses to a vertical list on mobile). Use it only where the business has genuine dated milestones — founded, licensed, an expansion, an Nth job, an award. If the business just "started in 2015" with nothing else to plot, omit the timeline — the prose + photo stand alone.
Slots: centered H2 origin heading · optional milestone timeline · origin prose beside a photo · a line naming the gap this business was started to fix. Leads into §A5.
